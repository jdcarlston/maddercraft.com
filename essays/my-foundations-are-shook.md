A couple days ago I decided there likely wasn’t going to be a better time to switch to Apple from Android. It’s been a long time coming and while I’m in flux I might as well take the opportunity to change systems to align with more of my family and my husband.

I got the phone at Verizon and downloaded the Android to Ios app. The man at the Verizon didn’t have me check the WhatsApp message transfer and that set me back because I lost my message history and had to go through a bunch of rigamarole to wipe and re-restore. The second time through it hung up and had to go through it again.

Third time was the charm.

Now it’s all learning a new interface, new motions, missing apps that don’t transfer, setting everything up with new two-factor auth, etc etc ad nauseum.

So…many…things. Things I didn’t anticipate both pleasant and not.

One thing I’ve run into is no more Twilight. It’s one of the most useful apps for not messing with my night sight and for blocking blue light. It seems to help me and my mental health. And Apple has a poor substitute. So I got Claude to build me, and others, a cheatsheet that gets me closer with an action button and accessibility settings.

* * *

The school year is winding down. My nephew had his field day and my youngest is graduating high school. It is wild and amazing they we are here. I’m so very proud of them and am excited to see them and see them graduate in a few short days.

I’ve been thinking a lot about my stint as a mom. As a human, and as a mom, I’m deeply aware of my flaws and at least some of my blindspots.

I often reflect on the ways I’ve caused my children and family pain. Life’s lessons aren’t always easy and I’ve definitely made my fair share of mistakes and poor decisions. I’ve also done everything I could to show my children love as best as I’ve known how. I know that’s a huge part of what my former and I have created together. The kids are alright. The world and it’s priorities for learning today are not going to be the same tomorrow and in the years to come. I hope I’ve modeled resilience and strength, open mindedness, compassion and boundaries – in the positive and the negative.

* * *

Last night I went to a transcendent experience of a concert at Underground Arts. Opening was Wayfarer, which was great.

Headlining though was Oranssi Pazuzu.

### This band saved my husband’s life.

It pulled him out of one of the darkest periods of his life. Much like Peaches did for me when I went through my divorce. The Peaches/Christeene tour was, prior to last night, my favorite concert ever. Small Venue, massive bass, deeeep angr and play and queer ridiculousness. And I got to meet and hug Peaches.

### Oranssi Pazuzu is one of the bands my husband and I deeply connected over.

When he introduced them to me and created a listening experience it caused me to fall hard. He gets it. This music is beyond the blackest of metal and it takes me places and touches me in places that are so tender and so hurt and so beauty and so raw and all the other things all at the same time. They are utter masters of their craft calling into other realms of experience and existence to bring forth what they do. Their music is absolutely architecture of the senses in such a profound way.

My heart. Oh my heart. Beyond words. And we ran into a couple of the band members on the way out. And we ended up with a chance to share a few words and some of our fangrrlawe at their mastery. I was dumbstruck and agog for the most part and likely said something less than well said.

Upon reflection, this band has shifted my consciousness in the most reality transurfing fundamental ways. They might have messed with my awareness enough to see the world around me so differently that I can’t go back to the way I was before.

Music and Art is not the same. Humanity is not the same from yesterday to today. I’m writing from another concert, which is great, And. Not the same.

There’s a leap that was made that has opened a completely different portal. What I must need to create is calling. Skill and measure matters. The creative cycle of meaning making matters. And it anti-matters.

Our ride back home to my sister’s place was stellar. I was riding on cloud nine and probably need aftercare after a scene like that. The insights into those places that haven’t been reached in so long hold power and seem to be bringing me into more complete resonance today around rightness and wrongness, what is black and what is white and what is grey silver rainbows.

You Don’t Get Pics.
