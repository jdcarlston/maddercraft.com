dmuzi

my guru my lover my spiritual friend

i heavent sceent you in so long

you teach me as the wildlands do

in each moment your wisdom

whispers like the wind

as it becomes a part of me

my body is a petri dish

my mind is a fish

slippery quick sand silver

split sliced scaled open

vulnerably signaling growth

in such spicy dicy bites

fabulous ways that lead me

like a master chef’s sushi

or a street urchin streetwise

to the knife’s edge

away back from you

my hell bound hearts love

my guru my lover my spiritual friend

tammuz
