I truly am Sorry,

The other kind

for the hurting

for the pain

For being unable

For trying so hard

to speak fully

For hanging in

For holding out

I love you

My own dear heart

with all my heart

Always Will

My choice

Thank you

I believe in you

Wondrously

I appreciate you

Perceptively

somuch

For all the times

Gifts

Please forgive me

for what I’ve done

And had to do

and not do

Thank you

For being there

when you were there

For loving me

how you could

For trying

as you did

I forgive you

For the hurt

For the pain

damagedone

mistakesmade

Please take care

Love yourself

Love all the parts

And all the pieces

No regrets

Please do the work

Face it all

Be as brave

As I know you to be

Find truth

Find anger

Find joy

Find tears

Cry loud

Cry long

Cry low and ugly

Search the dark

Search the light

Search the shards

And in-betweens

One and Another

Inside the twilight

Where stars turn

mirror round

Will find yourself

truelove

Again someday
