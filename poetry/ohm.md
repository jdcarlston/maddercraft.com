hum like a drum, like a bell, like a charm, like a chime.
hum like a drum, like a bell, like a charm, like a chime.

* * *

Ooo
Be with me. Be mine.

* * *

strum like a run, like a thumb, up the strut of your spine.
run like the rain, like a hand, moving down your side

* * *

I feel us vibrate.
Reverberate.

* * *

whine with a buzz, like a saw, like a drawling y’all.
whine with a buzz, like the drone of a cricket’s call

* * *

Be with me. Be mine.
Be with me. Be mine.

* * *

bang with a clang, like a gun, like a gang, on the town.
bang with a clang, like a shot, from the hand, of the law.

* * *

Ooo 
Be with me. Be mine.

* * *

I feel us vibrate.
Ooo

* * *

boom with a zoom, from a fall to the ground from on high
boom with a zoom, with a fall to the ground down low

* * *

Find me 
Seek me 
Free me
Leave me

* * *

Ooo

* * *
