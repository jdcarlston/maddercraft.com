Ridin' Roughshod
By the River
Feel my mount
Beneath me now

* * *

Ooo

* * *

There’s a lone wolf
Tired a huntin’
Watchin' us
Go slowly by

* * *

Sing a swansong
Guns akimbo
Feel the wind
Past me blow

* * *

Ridin’ Roughshod
By the River
Feel my mount
Beneath me now

* * *

Ooo

* * *

On the hillside
Rocks are fallin'
The clouds go
Runnin' by

* * *

Storms a comin'
And I'm longin'
For to rest
My weary hide

* * *

Yodeleyo
Yodeloo

* * *

Bones are tired
Mother's dead
And I got
Nowhere to go

* * *

I'm just lookin'
And just longin'
For some lovin'
In mah soul

* * *

Ridin’ Roughshod
By the River
Feel my mount
Beneath me now

* * *

I’m just lookin’
And just longin’
For some lovin’
In mah soul

* * *

Ooo Yodelee Yodelee Yodeloo
