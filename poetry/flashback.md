Moving forward

Healing now

Wholly thriving

Someway somehow

Writing stories

Not written yet

Finding pathways

Untaken unset

Empty vessel

Waiting Still

Filled with promise

Unfulfilled

Finding newness

Oldness too

Appreciation

Of all we do

Being doing

Being being

Being king

Being silent

Being me
