There once was a toothbrush

Made so delightfully

That it caused your mouth to feel

As if you'd eaten ambrosia

And lined your tongue with satin

This was no ordinary teeth cleaner

It had whiz bangs

And whamdamdoozles galore

And it was a small thing that

Could give pleasure

Even under duress
