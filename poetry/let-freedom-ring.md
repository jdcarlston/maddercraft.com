* * *

Let it Break
From my tolling heart

* * *

Let it Push
Wave after wave

* * *

Let it Sound
Crashing down

* * *

Let it Pound
Thumping clay

* * *

I am That I am!

* * *

Let it Pull
And erode from below

* * *

Let it Wash
What is loose out to sea

* * *

Let it Hold
And make space

* * *

For Those That Will

* * *

04/2019
