* * *

January Rain
falls like snow
but its not snow
life is change
especially now
in the anthropocene
where what was
does not appear
to be what is
the pace of change
leaves donut economics 
spinning like tops 
on a smooth summer
concrete sidewalk
that burns my feet
its an introduction
another initiation
into a powerful new
January Reality
