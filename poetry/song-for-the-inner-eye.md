Somewhere
Somewhere

* * *

In the ocean
In the sea
In my heart

* * *

Somewhere
Somewhere

* * *

In the ocean
In the sea
At the start

* * *

I have longed for
I have wanted
I have seen

* * *

Somewhere
Somewhere

* * *

Near the wildlands
Near the heartswell
You have been

* * *

Feel it deep
Feel it free

* * *

Somewhere
Somewhere

* * *

In the ocean
In the sea
In my heart

* * *
