Last night
You were in my dream
(not a nightmare)
Hale, God! and aware
You were The One
That remembers
And forgives
That cares
Is concerned
That understands
That loved
And wants to play someday
Remorseful
Still full of joy
We spoke
About our lives
And our regrets
You asked how I was doing
You cared goddammit
And I started to cry
Broke the connection,
Woke up
To feel tears
Streaming down my face
To feel the back of your hand
This time
Softly caressing my face
To hear you whisper
That you love me,
That you’re sorry,
In my ear

* * *

*It was a dream I had once. It was all me.*

* * *

*05/2019*
*Love doesn’t go away*
*It won’t just stop*
*Shifting solid in time*
*Redirects*
*Toward true love, myself*
