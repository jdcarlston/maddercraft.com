It’s time to wake up, dearest.
It’s time to wake up, dearest…?
It’s time to wake up!

* * *

We need to be out the door
I know you want to sleep more
I know it’s hard to move

* * *

We need to get ready
We need to get dressed
We need a morning routine

* * *

That’s more than just me yelling
That greets the day with song
That sets the tone of joy and love

* * *

School is starting… work is starting…
Its daylight time. Be ready!
I so much want you to feel good

* * *

There’s sun-a-shining
Rain-a-raining
Birds that sing “Hello”

* * *

Your dreams are done until the evening
Your body’s done with resting,
It’s terrible, I know

* * *

It’s time to wake up, dearest,
Move yourself,
Come on!!

* * *

Your brain needs waking
Your hands need grasping
Your feet need walking

* * *

There’s places to be
I long to show you
How I love you

* * *

Dearest,
Wake up, please…
Seize the day!
