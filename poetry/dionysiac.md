Madness dwells
in shrieking sounds

* * *

Brought urgent
by the bloodthirsty

* * *

Blown down
Ripped uprooted

* * *

Breaking chains
Chewing flesh

* * *

Liberating
Life

* * *

Light
Within

* * *

Close your eyes
Dark has come.

* * *
