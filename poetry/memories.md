reaching for tigers in the bassinet

looking up into her eyes as she hummed small tunes

her eyes so full of love

nursing in the wicker rocking chair

the first time she left

crying out-of-breath hard

magic tricks with coins and tennis balls

where did they go?

pulling them from an ear!

him walking on the barrel in the backyard

the sunlight streaming in

laughing, rolling inside

sneaking through the storerooms

watching people from above

exploring the attic, the dresses and toys

the smell of dust and mothballs

Grandma’s thimbles, so much fabric

the feel of the measuring tape as I’m fitted for dresses

picking fingernail paint from her vanity

marble machine

rolling-thunder down

records spinning and cassette tape stories and sounds

don’t bump or it’ll skip and scratch

sneaking out of class

playing with forbidden toys

swinging in the wind

stomach butterflies so high

bouncing on their knees

sleeping cuddled in their lap

peas and carrots and cornbread in the metal high chair

the car seat with the front that slid in

grocery carts pinched and fitted, poked by diaper pins

skiing down the sidewalk, down the hills

rolling in the grass

building snowmen and igloos in the yard

cutting my dolls hair and then my own

playing dress up, sewing clothes

so many stories and so many songs

sand pile next to Caesar barking with boundless energy

riding in the old green pick up truck

hot fudge, cold ice cream

grandpa in his flat cap and long coat

riding Big Red, so high up

crying when he died

climbing trees making nests

sandpile water works

floating so quiet in the middle of the pool

red-paint, flagstone, fancy new dining room

twin peaks mountains in the distance

crossing the bridge with poison ivy

breaking through the thistle

mounds of wild roses

thorny Russian olives smell so strong

cottonwood bundles blowing leaving falling

shooting baskets, picking strawberries

grapes and grape juice

bugs eggs, morning glory, dandelions blowing

spider webs sparkling in the garden

rat’s nests under the barn

snakes in the cracks

playing in the mud

riding horses in the rink

getting chased by Esmerelda the goose

chased by tumble weeds

climbing on the jungle jim, the wagon, the fence, the ropes,

smelling hay and grain and oats

guarded on the trampoline - one at a time

searching secret passageways

and sending boats and leaves and sticks and dreams downstream
