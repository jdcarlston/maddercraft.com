the sultry sun

rises

to tongue the sky

the wind

came and blew

the rain came too

rose and fell

lay quiet with

the earth

last evening

this morning

the clouds

passionately cuddle

sun capped

snow kissed

Kings Peak

that voyeur

sneaks a look

catches a glimpse

of a Knowing

like no other
