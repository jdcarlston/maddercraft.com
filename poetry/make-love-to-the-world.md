make love to the world
fall in love with existence

* * *

every moment
a sacred prayer

* * *

empty and silent
every moment filled

* * *

with bubbling laughter
erupting

* * *

[un]bidden
from your lips

* * *

make love
to the books you read

* * *

to the ones you’re with
to the one true self

* * *

the earth
the sea
the sky

* * *

and all you encounter
