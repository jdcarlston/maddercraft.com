Every word pours 
Slips slices like raindrops

fa(i)lls to fill our cups 
shift the scales of focus

how does One tell time
with Water

What’s felt is truth
that can’t be told

On nights when the Stars
and bodies will (scream)

meaning gets squashed
there are n0\* words

And when the flood is over
l@nd is in sight again

we’ve been Beyond }{ ourselves
Beyond the borders and bounds of Binah

And each [pair] of us needs 
Love and soothing tender kindness

Each of us needs To Form 
a chain a bond a knot of remembrance

Everything is okay
Everything will be okay

There are Many Worlds 
We can puddle jump to

Bunny Bunny Bunny
