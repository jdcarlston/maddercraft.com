golem I made you out of clay
to clay you now return

* * *

once a blessing upon our lips
now dust upon my shoes

* * *

too much magic hemmed within
a gordian knotted mind

* * *

you were beautiful my love
a turkish delight

* * *

\*\*\*

* * *

lobbed away from the beaten path
sounding off

* * *

your screams have been replaced
with didjer drone

* * *

resonating through my hall
on starry nights

* * *
