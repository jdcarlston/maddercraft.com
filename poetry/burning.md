what are we going to paint with our explosion

with our burning

with our churning

with.our.yearning

what are.we.going.to.blow

what.forging.to.burn

what are.we.going.breathe

what are we.going.to.exist.within

what are.we.going.to.choose

scientifically and.with purpose

what.is.a good.life lived?

coyotes should.yell.at.you and.say.wake.up

wake.up.from.your melbatoast.neighborhood

waiting for.the world.to.explode

we are all.together.emulsive.beige waiting.for.the.explosion

who the.fuck.cares

i'm.sorry

there.is.nothing
