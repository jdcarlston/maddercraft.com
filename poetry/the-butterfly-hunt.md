My cats, they fight
With butterfly knives
With butterflies
In the night.
My cars do too

* * *

Roaring up
On their hind wheels
Screeching “Ha Ha! I’ve got you now!”
But that’s more like a demolition derby
As opposed to a pussy fight

* * *

Well, that’s quite the image,
If you stop and think
Balanced on the wall,
Held at odds
Under the moon.
Fluttering in darkness

* * *

*06/2018*
