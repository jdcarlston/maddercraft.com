Last night I cried so hard it sounded like laughter
Last night the pain from the hunger and aching
Grew so much that I felt full, bliss

* * *

I saw an eternity of pain
I shook like a leaf and cinched my throat
I cried until my eyeballs shivered

* * *

I screamed when I got home
Into my pillow, loud and long against his rage
I wanted to go deaf and dumb

* * *

I wanted to hurt more than the hurt of this self-destruction
I cried at the sound, at the touch, I rode my horse backward
And it turned into laughing colors

* * *
