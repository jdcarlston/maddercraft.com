white veins of light
sparkle and prism
aquatic echoes
filtered sun

* * *

they didn't love me
like I loved them

* * *

stretches long
drink that in
gulp it down

* * *

expect nothing( l|n)ess
emptiness is the essence
of this Truth
this elegance

* * *
