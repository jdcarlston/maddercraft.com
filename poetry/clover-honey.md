* * *

she made me clover
honey sandwiches on toast
with butter and love

* * *

she taught me so much
modeled in grace each day
and let me run free

* * *

she showed me kindness
and strength in quietude
of learned persistence

* * *

she fully supported
my dreams and my wishes
in all things we shared

* * *

*202006*
