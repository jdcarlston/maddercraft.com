Make Love
Be Love
Do Love

* * *

Make love to the world
Fall in love with existence

* * *

Every moment is a sacred prayer

* * *

Empty and quiet

* * *

Every moment is filled

* * *

With bubbling laughter
Erupting unbidden from your lips
Your soul
With tears dripping from your eyes
With song that rages against the dying of the light

* * *

Feel it now
Feel it here

* * *

Make Love
Be Love
Do Love

* * *

To the books you read
To the stories you tell
The people you’re with
To what you find

* * *

Within your heart

* * *

The earth
The sea
The sky

* * *

All you encounter

* * *

Emptiful

* * *

Beautiful

* * *

Ugly and Ashamed

* * *

Dark
Light

* * *

Free

* * *

Let it be Love
Let it be Loved
Let it be Lover

* * *

*102021*
