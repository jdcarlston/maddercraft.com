A trajectory outward, never crossed.
Between the two and ten

* * *

A time for me and a rhyme for you
A meter that never quite matched.

* * *

Hours apart at opposite ends
With dust-blown storms across

* * *

Unless you go the long way home
Moving eight hours round the bend

* * *

04/2019
