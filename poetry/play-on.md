* * *

A chord is struck
The dance begins
The light plays
Around the room

The beat skips
The ball falls
And everyone’s
Feeling deflated

The silence moves
Like a zombie
Shuffling between
The faces in time

And a smile says
“Come play with me,
See the lights”
The music revs up again
