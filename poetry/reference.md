You might refer to me as
Fucking Cunt.
(Fucking Bitch as well — time and time again.)

* * *

While other expletives may be added -
for enhanced understanding, mind you,
there's nothing so sweet as being called Cunt.

* * *

My holy holes hath offended thee.
Damn spots stain your mind.
Steam and spray all you like. It will stay.

* * *

To not be yours. To not be shut. In truth we flow.
I am my own. I have my own. I will mine own.
Name me so lovely.

* * *

For I so love the world.
And it makes sweet love
Back to me.
