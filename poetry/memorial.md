Boreal clouds hold a dim sun
Bodies blown in ebb and flow

* * *

Spring-clean rain pelts our skin
Eyes seeing myth and truth

* * *

Planets line up, lighting the sky
A waning moon, waxing eloquent

* * *

Welcomed us home

* * *

Greeting us through the garden trees
At the end of each Hand-held path

* * *

The meandering water flows 
Hot and cold

* * *

We sat enthroned Between our shadows
Spinning yarns Around the starfire

* * *

A fallen nymph bows 
Her heavy head, stumped

* * *

Napping in her cousin’s shaded heart
Waking to paint Underwater pearls

* * *

Wayne, king of black cats calls, 
Commanding homage for the gifts

* * *

Turning us mere mortals
Into wrinkled fishfolk

* * *

Clouds framing the Moon 
Into a feathered memory

* * *

Whispered “This should be the end.”
Apart from the named and nameless others

* * *

Before waverushing closer
Felt us Slipdrifting away

* * *

We were gods
And goblins

* * *

My heart cried out at the shift
Felt the undercurrent go cold

* * *

Dug my Heels in as they called to Stay
My head Flew off cawing “Leave!”

* * *

Refusing, 
I Exploded that day

* * *

Decoupled in the center of that pool

* * *
