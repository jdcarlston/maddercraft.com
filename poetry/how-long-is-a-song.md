How long is a song and who is it for?

What is its measure and what is its score?

How long did it take to practice at night?

Did it seep, did it slip, did it slam you up right?

Did it run through your mind, your dreams, or your soul?

Did it empty you out or leave your heart full?

Did it wash you away, once and for all?

How does it feel, do you recall?
