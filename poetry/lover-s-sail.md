All that's left

Are the winds of fancy

That I used to buoy

My love

Between the storms

The cobwebs

And dust I used to

Heal the gashes

Seal the leaks

And hold it

Together

In the cold

And lonely

Waters

I miss my boat

I miss my lover

Shipmate

Lost at sea

Oh captain

My captain

I am

My own captain

I wanted to

Go down with

The ship

But that ship

Has sunk

Has sailed

* * *
