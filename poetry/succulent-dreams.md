Lying down delicious deep in sweetest slumber
Running roughshod with a pounding heart
Aching softly slid between your limbs
Moving up and down your spine
Long for that someone
Sleeping far away
Far and far
Away

* * *

Lips
Throbbing
Pursing pursuit
Tongue almost tasting
Eyes feeling for an eager face
Hands slip beneath the blankets
Drawing you close closer to their side
Breathing in unison one heart one mind

* * *
