…Don’t know the full story

Or all the implications

manipulations

Just the aftermath

Of how I know this

Of what *actually* happened

It was a feeling

A sense

An impression

A flash of intuition

A blow

That finally let me see

Where the damage

Originated

In your past

And the fact that

You made your choice

To keep it hidden

For safety keeping

I couldn’t stay

It hurt too much

Each time

I saw your face

Felt it

Couldn’t wait

Longer

Swing batter

Too slow

I know

Three strikes

Can only hope

Root-caused

A look deeper

God the hurt

Shown all along

It was all

You

Something always

Was wrong

Hidden

Forgotten

And I left

There was

No stay

Knocked out to

See it through

Not after what happened

For looping

Into being killed

Of you

MaKing good

On your threats

Of being

Locked away

Not being

There

Because that's

What had happened

To you

As a child

Came Too close

To the Truth

Someday

The sun will come up

On an endless plane of night

Dancing

To infinity and beyond…
