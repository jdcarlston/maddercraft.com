Hard work

Smart work

kick kick kick

Chin up

Shoulders back

jab punch uppercut

uppercut punch punch jab

run run

go go go

YOU CAN DO IT

DON’T GIVE UP

* * *

*Remember for the future: When your life is in danger, it’s best not to pull punches or hold back. Don’t wait. Act! Get out of there! Leave as soon as the tide turns.*
