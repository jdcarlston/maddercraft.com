* * *

On and off and on and off
Zip-smoosh-zip-smack

New-age material
Modeled from nature

Developed for flights
In the vacuum of space

Helps you run faster
Grip stronger

And with a burred hitch
Pulled askew, detaches

At your feet and fingertips
Over and over

Bundled, adhered, Until
Fuzz rips, Hooks strip
