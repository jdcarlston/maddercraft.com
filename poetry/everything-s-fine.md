this moment is fine
even though it smells
a bit like burnin'

* * *

this moment is fine
even though my heart
is cracked open

* * *

this moment is fine
even though my baby's
cryin'

* * *

this moment is fine
even though the world
keeps turnin'

* * *

this moment is fine
cause, so far,
i keep breathin'

* * *
