last nights Dreams
rolled in like mountain Clouds
heard that Witch wail
echoing deep within a cave
tolling Weave been longing to hear

* * *

The Apology.

* * *

spoken silently sideways
felt long lived within your lips
herd out between breathless teeth
spoke it rolled upon your tongue
bitten back snorts trampling down
kissed the coinage hand to mouth
cloven hooves dug deep for moss and lichen
suckling upon the musky earth

* * *

mind deep within the longest vein
struck gold and cached it forth
sacrificed the lambs breath bonds
bilt around the core
voiced and breathing energy
felt the basest one release
like heaven knocking at the door
seeing you again
the real believed in you
loving you wholly regarding
who you truly are

* * *

Oh God! This Thou Art.

* * *

like a falcon keening feeling free again
speaking to myself indigenous
sparkling eyes white with clarity dark with wisdom

* * *

Gtong Len
Rigpa

* * *

traveled upward this side of the waterfall
living on the leeward bend of the kestrel peak
black yak shelter fashioned *rTse-sbra*
work hard won in iced thin air and muggy sweat

* * *

whether in the snowy heights
or foggy river bottoms
there we guided as an innocent child
through Landscapes scarred
with pockmarks like the back of a Hand

* * *

heart of my inner World
high mountain Air
swirling Love like Snow
whistling whooping whispering
in name an Action with ever much to do
holding on dear with tenacity letting go
shooting Love and pistolled Pain
as she looked into my eyes
she quietly spoke her real name
wrote it out in longform
on the palm of my hand

* * *

གཏོང་ལེན

* * *

left a tingling sense felt Just… there
true naming of this spirits land
small stature calmed presence bright and crooked smile
that which keeps on going
Giving Taking to those what will
you apologized to her, unblocked in full
revert the diverting of the rivers source
her they my breath received and sent
that support and led
in the far and summit North
*beyul* hidden lands
the uncharted waterfalls of my mind

* * *

now I can begin

* * *

*To you who apologized I forgive. To you who I’ve harmed I’m sorry. May I take all your hurts and pains and suffering. May I transmute them as the sorrow of my heart. May they be released back into the earth from which they rose as pure energy.*

*I shall hold you rare and regard you as the most* [*sacred friend*](https://www.success.com/what-the-dalai-lama-taught-me-about-relationships/)*. For without my sacred friends I would never have had to evolve my heart to be bigger than the pain they bring.*
