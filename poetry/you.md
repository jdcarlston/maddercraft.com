*This is a song I wrote for someone I loved with all my heart. And yet, looking back, it was poorly and imperfectly, as we all do.*

*I lost myself in holding tight. Gently child, gently! Love me better, now.*

*What a piece of work is man!*

*When I sing this song, it represents the love and appreciation I’ve been hoping for all my life. It has been a gift for the deepest dark within me to see what love is and where it shines brightest.*

*I will only accept kind treatment from myself and others. I will no longer make myself available to those who are cruel, controlling, or inconsiderate.*

*I wish all the people who are no longer a part of my life the best. I hope you grow in ways that support yourself and those you care for.*

*I thrive. Love is felt without measure, freely given, and earned.*
