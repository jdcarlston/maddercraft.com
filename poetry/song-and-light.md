The song
Of your voice
As you fall asleep
The light
On your face
Waking up
In the morning
As you look away
Get up.
Get ready.
For bed.

* * *

The shift
Of your muscles
As you move
From space to space
The little things
I notice
Appreciate
As new
Each day a changin'
Kaleidoscope.
Of time.
Of color.

* * *

With purpose
Precious precision
Noted and loved
The fold
Of your elbow
The shape
Of your words
Wet
Or dry.
Lilting Smiles.
Grating Frowns.

* * *

Acting and inert
Conflicted and sure
All sprung
Radiantly resplendent
Catching the glimpses
The range
Of interpretation
Light and dark.
Brown and pink.
Green or grey.

* * *

The big events
In our lives
The mundane moments
I want you
In the pictures
And the songs,
To live it all
The structures
Of our lives.
Exactly as I am.
We are.
Ever-changing.

* * *
