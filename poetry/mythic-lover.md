Has a face I know

»—» Melting with compassion

A growling sound

— » Asking patient questions

Cracked lips

— » Mouthing speech that softens

Mind riddled with mazes

— » Listening intently

Legs running

— » Next to, upon, around

Hands reaching to pull

— » Gently closer

Eyes charged

— » With paired desire

A slice

— » Of everything
