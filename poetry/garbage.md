it’s time 
to deal with the refuse
that got thrown
onto the earthen floor beside my energy body
By those unintegrated young shy of or sinkwell of spirit
Living inside 
those universes whom i thought i used to know

* * *

black holes that chewed and threw away everything
time and stories told and retold roughly flung through film projector eyes
far flung twisting winding wounding torched and lit 
flickering shade and stark value attempting to shatter the peace 
while the galaxies within try to win a zerosum game no one else was playing
Pictures of Gods and Monsters 
Orion’s hare hung tongue tied together on the hunter’s belt

* * *

broken shards of stars like pottery
strewn about the Abyss
what little leftover evidence remains debris
lessons that werent learned were let go
the art of repair undone 
Gone are the bindings necessary to secure a Goldilocks Zone
a garden of love a forest of care a meadow of joy

* * *

oh Lordess forgive us all
for no one knows what we do 
forcing the break for the ache to repair the wear broke
unrealized inquiry dulled the wits embattled within
lies hiding from those deepest voices and kindest selves
Sopping up an unreconciled soup of justification
processing parbaked breadcrumb ideas around a strifeful fire

* * *

acknowledge the sacred mess thats left behind 
remains of poverty of heart and mind
the contract was met was planned outside time
the end came and went without coming
yes its time briefly time this now until later
To clean up the scraps that were left
from the fits that dont fit within this kith and kin life

* * *

“***It’s not You that caused the harm, it’s their understanding of you that caused the harm*.** You are an honest and good person, with positive intentions and a genuine heart.

Part of growing up is accepting the limitations of the people you love and loving them anyway.

Adulthood is not rejecting people, going to the other side of the room, or cutting people off. It’s finding the resources and center of calm within yourself, regardless of what others do or don’t do, regardless of whether they are meeting your needs or not, and understanding their limitations and accepting them.

This allows you to meet them outside of anger, to remain calm in the face of other’s storms and offer assistance. You can understand that the other person is poverty stricken, and because you’re spiritually abundant and have all the resources you need, you can give where they are lacking.

This is also what Jesus meant when he said ‘Love Your Enemies.’ It’s a very bizarre thing to say. But in order to love your enemies you must be so abundant, so far ahead of your enemies it does not hurt you to give that love back to them where they need it.”

— Paraphrasing Dr. Robert Dee McDonald

i would add that this is evidence that perhaps the ascended masters know that to love your enemies was to love yourself, others, and the world more deeply — allowing all parties a chance to rupture, repair, and thus strengthen one’s hearts-kept inner peace. And, truly, we have no inner enemies, only inner friends.

Thank you dear heart for showing me externally what i could not fully see in my ignorance.
