* * *

Bursting pain —
Fireworks

* * *

Fly straight into
A vantablack mirror
Behind my eyes

* * *

Flash! Bang!

* * *

Events and scenes
Reflect in droplets
Tear and wash away

* * *

Candle wax
Puddling formless

* * *

Colored chalk
Dissolving on a sidewalk

* * *

Glass shards
Buried at a cross roads

* * *

*04/2019*
