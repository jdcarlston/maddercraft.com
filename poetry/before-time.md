before the Universe
 — formed

before Space-Time-Matter 
 — existed

before Being was being 
 — nothing

conceived in 
 — itself

born of emptiness 
 — and always

breathing out— and in

split in two 
 — other

in conversation with 
 — everything

and naught
 — brought about something

in loving 
 — disagreement

in creative
 — imbalance

breaking into 
— all

out of and inside 
— attraction

force spinning
 — orbiting whole
