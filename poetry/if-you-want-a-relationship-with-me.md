You’d better respect my “No."

If you want a relationship 
that fills a hole 
learn what it takes to move mountains

If you want a relationship 
that feeds your soul 
then learn to cook and clean

If you want a relationship 
that lasts longer than it takes to walk away 
then learn to take it slow

Show some self-control.
It’s not a race. It’s not a show. 
And trust isn’t built by grabbing my ass.

My attention is not to be conquered 
Nor am I a trophy to be won
What you get is gifted or none

Learn to speak to act from within 
your kindest self 
before you work with me

If you want a relationship
then Build your life yourself around 
the art of an emphatic “Yes!”
