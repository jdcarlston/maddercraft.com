There was a time,
At the deepest part of myself,
That I believed
I was not alone

* * *

It was a beautiful thing.

* * *

I have never put
My everything into something
So deeply. Heart-centered.
Can’t imagine it now

* * *

ALONE! ALONE! ALONE!

* * *

Watching you
My lover
Clawing, climbing, throwing
Kicking, spitting, grasping
Pushing, Pulling, Shaking

* * *

ALONE! ALONE!

* * *

Watching you
My lover
Blind and gulping
Fish out of water
Deadened eyes.

* * *

Watching you
My lover
Heart pains, sharp heavy tight
Skin taut, yellowed, gray
Cracked and dry

* * *

Watching you
My lover
Reddened-deadened-eyed
Sagging tired awake and sweating
On all fours, dead man’s pose

* * *

ALONE!

* * *

What does it mean when
I find black and blue bruises,
Stamping violence everywhere,
Placed strategically by you
My lover
Running further than my eyes can see?

* * *

I could have run, far away
And yet I stay
I see I hear I taste I touch
I felt so much
In too great a silence

* * *

Thank gods I’m Alone.
