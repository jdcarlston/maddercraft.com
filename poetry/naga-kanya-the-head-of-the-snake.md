Ah-sa-sa-ra

I am that Feminine

Who Holds

The Head of the Snake

In her hands

That calls the conch

With bellow blows

That bites the Psyche

And sucks the marrow

Who crawls like a crab

Looks it in the eye

And devours it whole

Destroying the wyrm

That once bit the apple

Before the Fall

* * *

*06/2018*

*The Sibyl called upon Naga Kanya to help the wounded wonder child one fateful summer night after our bodies and soul were now-and-future violated by the demon pretenders. For they truly loved-hated us in our radiance and worth and could abide us no longer.*

*She, the naga, is that crab-walking snake-slithering protectoress that will come to save us from ourselves, from our choices, and our future. If her gift had not been rescinded by the wonder child, she, very readily, would have saved us all much pain.*

*As it was, when later we were all called forward to save the child’s light we did what was right. Given the furthest essential and loving choice, we fought dearly and lived with great sacrifice . We lost the good mother in deference to her frightened child. We mourn her death and celebrate her life as if she were us, a queen, ourselves.*
