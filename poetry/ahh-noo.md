* * *

Another night crying in bed
Getting fewer and farther between

* * *

Missing. Thinking Lover.
Missing. Thinking Over.
Missing. Empty aching.

* * *

Sweeps through.
Clutching.

* * *

It was just a dream.
There’s no running away.

* * *

From the truth
Of this pain.

* * *

true love
supports
the inner self

hearts
