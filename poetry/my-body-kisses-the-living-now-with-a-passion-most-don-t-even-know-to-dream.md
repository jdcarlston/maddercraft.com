my body
kisses the Living Now
with a passion
most don’t even know
To Dream

with each moment
that passes
Eternity tongues
the crevasses 
of my mind

coaxing and tickling
the underbelly
that floats within
the river flow
of Awareness

biting through
the thighs of Belief
after being roasted
on the spit
of Imagination
