* * *

let my heart break
let my heart break
let my heart break

* * *

and keep breaking
open
breaking into billions

* * *

of tiny pieces
and those pieces keep breaking
as an everlasting earthquake in side me

* * *

until they are atomized — ized
and my pain, my pleasure
dis s s s solves re solves

* * *

into the background radiation
the vibrating gong of the universe
singing Ohm

* * *

into every multiversal mitochondrial strand
reaching backward forward
in space time

* * *

until i transform
Oh! to hold a new aware ness
cuddling with what love really is

* * *

so that I learn
what it is like
to ex x x x pan d

* * *

x pressing a changing
understanding of now
and them and self and other

* * *

and how
it all relates
within the flow

* * *

let my heart break into
what a truly 0pen
heart feels

* * *
