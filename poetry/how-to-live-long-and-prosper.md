* * *

Volunteer in a food kitchen, better yet, donate food and make a meal
Donate blood, better yet, swab a cheek and donate marrow
Be a friend, better yet, become a friend to those in pain
Donate money, better yet, schedule the ten percent
Require respect, better yet, live and teach it
Learn a skill, better yet, work at mastery
Play music, better yet, perform it
Make art, better yet, share it
Ask questions, better yet,
Listen

* * *

*Practicing effective empathy can help you to reduce your personal stress, live a longer and more rewarding life and make the world a better place for yourself and others. Putting it into practice is a gloriously selfish and altruistic measure that puts a laser focus on your effort to affect life on sweeping scale.*
