All this Love with nowhere to go

I’m sending it blindly outward

I imagine you, your smile

I dream of you, your presence

And the things we’ve done together

And apart

I miss you, all the things you are,

And aren’t

Your face, your eyes, your touches

I want you to be my person

And to seek me in return

I am waiting

To see what’s happening

And it wakes me up at night

Knocking on my own locked door

Of my home, of my hotel room

Leaking through all my openings

How full can one broken heart be
