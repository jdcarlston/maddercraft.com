wherein *always*
and *never* reside
and *forever* *and ever* exist
*nothing* is right
and it's *all* gone wrong

* * *

*failure*
*not enough*
*everyone*’s bad — the lies of victimhood
living death
racing thoughts

* * *

in the *narrows*
*beneath* a puddle
that was once us
*trapped* in the infinities
*constricting* the mind

* * *

accept it
listen well and full
dive deep
go long go wide
to secure our release

* * *

reforming
out of mud
of muck reborn
tear-dried kiln-fired
into the wild

* * *

into the lands of *maybe*
*tomorrow*
*could be*
*perhaps*
and *possibly*

* * *

*solve et coagula*

* * *

*202012*
