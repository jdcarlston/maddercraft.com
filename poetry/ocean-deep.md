Sea Mother
Sea Mother

Look at me with wonder
Look at me truly long

(Sea Mother)
(My Mother)

With your ocean eyes
What see you Sea Mother?
(Mother)

What do you find in me?

(Sea Mother)

* * *

My child
Oh child

In you I find wonder
And awe as deep as can be
Proud of you, my dearest
Hearts Swell
(My child)
My hearts swell with pride
As I watch you walk 
You walk through the sea

My arms long to hold you
To hug you 
And squeeze you
To keep you close by my side

(My child)
(Oh child)

Know my dear, my darling
With reckless wild I feel
Loving truly deeply
Means seeing you
Seeing you to the sky

* * *

To fly to fly
fly fly fly

* * *
