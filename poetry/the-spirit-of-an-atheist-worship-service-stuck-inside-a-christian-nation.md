this world
can still be

worshipped

without coercion command or control

without the bull
without the cross
without the trappings

rather …

with informed consent

chance existence
the movement of the stars

can be held sacred
whispered in jubilance
of the incredible odds

wrapped in random rapture
by creedless canon

written by rites
that speak in reverence
to the secular

raised and gilded gleaming
by the senses within

each body
each organ
each individual journey

the luscious fruit

offered

by personal sacrifice

even as we fall victim

to our own aged fragility
to our own busy gusto

intent and focused

with living devotion to

our eyes
our ears
our touch
our smell

and taste

our intuition

and our mind

can find ritual
in careful observation
and solemn practice

in thoughtful repose

trained response
guided by
the astounding imagination

express our silent prayers

through compassionate care

kindness

done to others
and self without fear
of hell to goad us

we can be held
in communion with

each act
each friend
each spoken word

be spelled

and every choice
full filled

we … make

we … create

we … will

beyond belief

in something
in nothing

other

than the magic

of existence

* * *
