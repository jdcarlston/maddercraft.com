* * *

picked it up joggled gently
felt its weight and measure
gazing at the giver’s eyes
guessing at its contents
with graceful recognition
fingers peeled its wrapping
shucked, held up, cooed over
passed around and shared
giving thanks for that before us

* * *

*for Mom*
