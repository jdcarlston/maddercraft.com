Long Beards, Bloody Marys, and Ship-wreck tattoos
Ahoy matey!

Whiskey and EVOO saturate the heirloom roasted tomatoes
Local fresh

Farm-to-Table frontmen shilling
Ecksss-pee-ree-yence

Gose (pronounced goes-eh) going gone in a briny brim of bro-power
Check it!

The ukulele strums a sublimating serenade
Tweet Tweet

The twang of goat-cheese “whatnots” congeal around 
Blushing Beets

Pumping nuts to up the protein of the overworked Don’t-Be-Evils
Pub Crawl

For the hoppiest next-best-right thing in the Sssssstream
Lit

By an Android’s crypto-White stare
YOLO!
