* * *

i will greet you
at the end
without fear
with love
held close
ringing in
an empty vessel
waiting
in fullness
of time
i'll meet you
there now
out beyond
right and wrong
away from the things of man
oh! love
let us
away from the things of man
where all round
the other side
yawns nothing
resting quiet between the roar
the eye inside our sound and light

* * *

Yours Truly
