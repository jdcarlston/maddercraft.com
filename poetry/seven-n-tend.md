All the tender intention I had
S’even — 7 — & — Septimus

* * *

All that I set upon your brow
All the intention in the world

* * *

Couldn't have changed
What You Believed

* * *

Or how you treated me
Or what happened

* * *

I just wish
I ended it
Sooner Now

* * *

You never were
Who you said
You are.

* * *

Truth
I can see that
Bigger Now

* * *

My heart
My head
My being

* * *

Cries out
For my own
False beliefs

* * *

Left in the cold
Hard Truths
Of what is

* * *

Waking up without
Your arms
You gentlest love
Your softest kisses
Your biggest lies

* * *

Tending toward me
Turned out
It was
All mine
Own creation
How lonely
And empowering
Fantastic

* * *

*A tiger can’t change its spots*
*And dragons fly alone*

* * *

04/2019
