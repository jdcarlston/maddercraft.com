coyotes howl me awake

on all+saints morning

the clock reads 3

i’m to attend a funeral today

as part of a rainbow of grim reapers

brunching on the grass

outside the family clinic

for one of our fallen mothers

i don’t want to forget my scythe

*2021/11*
