Energy spreads like blood
Veined through cells,
Nerves and muscle,
Bones and skin.

A connection rooted
To the anxious-heavy core,
A palpable pit that
Distributes a bug,

Once a necessary system hack
Placed there long ago
When survival coerced
The structure into being

The polymorphic tendrils
Twist and turn in time
Hiding in the code that infects,
Chipping at the boot sector of my mind.

I wake with spinning cycles
Thoughts that mis-assign identifiers
Pointing to fried memory
Threads spawn around deadlocked processes

The engine revs, searching for terms
Sorting through the probabilities
Of the Bayesian subjective
I find nothing, while looping
