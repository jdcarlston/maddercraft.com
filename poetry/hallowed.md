musk

pours crisp

from the southwest

sun dusting pale

through orangered oak

leaves dancing flames

whispering paper mache

secrets left over

summer storms blown

past crypted nares

swollen tender through

such mazed hallways

sweeping sloughs

torrid smoke rings

swirling tori

dryly snorting

inhaling deaths

dewed perfume
