## On the writing desk

*The Whores Grimoire* is a book of essays and workings written mostly as Roz Madder. It follows Ma'at, the old Egyptian sense of balance and truth, from the heights of the Tree down into the world we live in, and holds that this world, imperfections and all, is **holy**.

Among the first pieces: what it means to see, hear and know with the heart, as the Egyptians described it, and the *Tale of the Eloquent Peasant*, whose plea for justice weighs the tongue and the heart on Ma'at's scale.
